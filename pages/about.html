Connecting Communities to Rescue Local Food

Every single day, incredible local restaurants, bakeries, and markets throw away tons of perfectly delicious food simply because it didn't sell before closing time. At the same time, community members are facing rising food prices and searching for affordable, high-quality dining options nearby. This disconnect leads to millions of pounds of unnecessary waste, lost revenue for small business owners, and missed opportunities for residents to support their local neighborhood economy.

Main Street Eats bridges this gap by creating an instant, real-time marketplace that connects hungry locals with surplus items from neighborhood food establishments at a fraction of the original price. By providing a seamless digital platform for daily food rescue, we promise to help small businesses recover lost costs, make quality food accessible to everyone, and drastically cut down local food waste—one meal at a time.
