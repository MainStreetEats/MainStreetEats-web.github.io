Our Services

Main Street Eats provides a platform connecting local food establishments with community members to reduce daily food waste. Our core services include:

  • Daily Surplus Alerts: Notifications when local bakeries and eateries post discounted surplus items.
  • Partner Dashboard: Simple inventory management for restaurants to list end-of-day specials.
  • Community Pickup Scheduling: Easy reserve-and-collect options for nearby meals.
